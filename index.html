Q2: RE2=50, RC2=200
قدم ۱ — KVL1
↻ KVL1
15 − 0.7 = IB1·(150k + 151×0.3k) − IB2·0.3k
14.3 = IB1·195.3k − IB2·0.3k
قدم ۲ — KVL2
↻ KVL2
15 − 1.4 = IB1·150k + 151·IB2·0.05k
13.6 = IB1·150k + IB2·7.55k
قدم ۳ — حل ماتریسی
| 14.3 | = | 195.3k −0.3k | × | IB1 |
| 13.6 | | 150k 7.55k | | IB2 |
det = 195.3k × 7.55k − (−0.3k) × 150k
det = 1,474,515,000,000 + 45,000,000,000 = 1.5195×10¹² = 1519.5M

IB1 = (14.3 × 7.55k + 0.3k × 13.6) / 1519.5M
IB1 = (107.965k + 4.08k) / 1519.5M = 73.74 µA

IB2 = (195.3k × 13.6 − 150k × 14.3) / 1519.5M
IB2 = (2656.08k − 2145k) / 1519.5M = 336.3 µA
قدم ۴ — IC ها
IC1 = 150 × 73.74µ = 11.06 mA
IC2 = 150 × 336.3µ = 50.45 mA
قدم ۵ — چک VCE
VCE1 ≈ 15 − 0.3k×(11.13 − 0.336) ≈ 11.77 V ✅
VCE2 ≈ 15 − 50.45m×0.25k ≈ 2.39 V ✅
IB1=73.74 µA، IB2=336.3 µA، IC1=11.06 mA، IC2=50.45 mA
